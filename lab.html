A Studio Built for

Our purpose-built studio in MGM Nagar, Tamil Nadu spans over 2,000 square feet of professionally designed photography space. Every corner has been crafted with one goal in mind: creating the perfect environment for capturing extraordinary images.

From the seamless white cyclorama wall in our main portrait studio to the dedicated makeup and styling suite, from the professional Godox and Elinchrom lighting rigs to our Fujifilm DL600 colour lab — every element reflects our obsession with quality.

Our Spaces

Six Distinct Studio Zones

Every zone is purpose-designed for a specific type of session — from bridal suites to printing labs.

Studio 1
Main Portrait Studio

Our flagship 800 sq ft portrait studio featuring seamless white and grey cyclorama walls, professional 4-point lighting rigs, and a wide range of backdrops and props.

Garden Studio
Studio 2
Outdoor Garden Area

A lush, landscaped outdoor garden perfect for natural-light portraits, pre-wedding shoots, maternity sessions and children's photography.

Bridal Suite
Studio 3
Bridal Suite & Makeup Room

An elegant, private bridal preparation room with professional Hollywood mirror lighting, dedicated makeup station and a comfortable waiting lounge for the wedding party.

Print Lab
Lab 1
Printing & Framing Lab

Our climate-controlled printing lab houses the Fujifilm DL600, Epson SC-P5000 large format plotter, professional framing equipment and a museum-quality album binding station.

Editing Suite
Suite 1
Editing & Retouching Suite

A sound-proofed editing suite with Apple Mac Studio M2 Ultra workstations, colour-calibrated 5K Retina displays and professional Lightroom and Photoshop workflows.

Client Lounge
Lounge
Client Viewing Lounge

A comfortable, beautifully furnished lounge where clients can view their edited gallery on a 55" 4K display, choose prints and approve final designs in a relaxed atmosphere.
