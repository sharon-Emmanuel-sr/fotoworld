Meet Our Team

Five passionate professionals dedicated to the art of visual storytelling.

With 20+ years of uncompromising artistry, Rajan has built FOTO WORLD from a single-room studio into Tamil Nadu's premier photography destination. His keen eye for light, emotion and composition has produced work that has moved thousands of families to tears of joy.

A self-taught artist turned industry pioneer, Rajan founded FOTO WORLD in 2004 with a vision to bring world-class photography to Tamil Nadu. His work has been featured in leading Indian wedding publications, and he has trained 50+ photographers over his career.

Specialising in emotional storytelling and cinematic light, Rajan approaches each wedding as a director approaches a film — with meticulous preparation, creative vision and a deep respect for every cultural tradition.
